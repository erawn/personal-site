title: 'Notebooks on Demand'
excerpt: 'Computational Notebooks are great programming environments for understanding and exploring a program, but have problems with large files and codebases. Instead of trying to ''fix'' notebooks, we redesigned the *relationship* between notebooks and IDEs, embedding a dynamically generated **Notebook on Demand** (NOD) in an IDE environment. Over ~4 weeks, five programmers reported how they used NOD to understand their programs (including those generated with AI), explore new ideas in a temporary environment, and write functions while still being able to compose code interactively.'
