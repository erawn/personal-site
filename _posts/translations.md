title: 'Exploring the Translation-As-Explanation Folk Wisdom for Program Editing Tasks'
excerpt: 'A common intuition is that programmers working with unfamiliar libraries or languages would benefit from seeing translations into familiar languages. Do translations actually help? We conducted a 7-condition, 98-participant user study. Surprisingly,there was a 67%–89% chance that translations alone made participants less likely to complete program modification tasks correctly.'
